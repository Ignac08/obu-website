## Obedience Bootcamp Level 1 class in Mission

Our Obedience Bootcamp is for beginners or experienced dog owners looking to build a strong relationship with their dog and practise obedience in a group setting. Mission classes are held at our Silverdale facility, 9221 Woodward St, on the south end of the Maple Ridge / Mission border, with a 4,000 sq ft covered arena so classes run rain or shine.

Dogs from Mission, Maple Ridge, Pitt Meadows, Abbotsford and across the Lower Mainland are welcome.
