## Obedience Bootcamp Level 1 class in Chilliwack

Our Obedience Bootcamp is for beginners or experienced dog owners looking to build a strong relationship with their dog and practise obedience in a group setting. Classes are taught by our head trainer Deb at 6933 Chadsey Rd, on our 5-acre property in Greendale on the Abbotsford / Chilliwack border.

Dogs from Chilliwack, Abbotsford, Agassiz, Hope and across the Fraser Valley are welcome.
