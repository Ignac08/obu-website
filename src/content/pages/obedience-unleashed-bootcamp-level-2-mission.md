## Obedience Bootcamp Level 2 in Maple Ridge and Mission

Build on the skills learned in Obedience Bootcamp Level 1 to achieve the next level of obedience while working through real-life scenarios. You learn to introduce toys as a reward and a motivator, expanding your training toolbox.

Classes are held at our Silverdale facility, 9221 Woodward St, on the Maple Ridge / Mission border.

**Prerequisite:** successful completion of Obedience Unleashed Obedience Bootcamp Level 1 or Puppy Class.
