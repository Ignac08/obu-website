### Admission & Vaccination Requirements
To keep all dogs safe and healthy, Obedience Unleashed requires proof of up-to-date vaccinations prior to your dog’s first visit. The following vaccinations are required for all doggie daycare and boarding guests:

- **Rabies** — required for all dogs
- **DHPP** (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, Parainfluenza) — required for all dogs
- **Bordetella** (kennel cough) — optional, but requires a 3-week wait period if you choose to receive it before you can attend our facility.

We accept all dogs, including intact dogs; however, intact dogs are unable to participate in group daycare play with other intact dogs.

Puppies are welcome starting at 4 months, and upon completion of the current full puppy vaccine series.

Please bring a copy of your dog’s vaccination records on the first day of drop-off, or email them in advance.

## FAQ

### What vaccinations does my dog need for daycare?
All dogs must be up to date on Rabies and DHPP vaccines prior to their first visit. Bordetella is optional, but requires a 3-week wait period if you choose to receive it before coming to Obedience Unleashed. Proof of vaccination is required on or before the first day of drop-off.

### At what age can my puppy start daycare?
Puppies are welcome once they have completed their full puppy vaccination series, typically around 4 months of age. Please bring proof of completed vaccinations on the first visit.

### Does my dog need to be spayed or neutered?
We accept all dogs at our facility, including intact dogs; however, intact dogs are unable to participate in group daycare play with other intact dogs.

### How do you handle aggressive or reactive dogs?
The safety of every dog and staff member is the top priority. If a dog displays aggressive or reactive behaviour toward staff, Obedience Unleashed reserves the right to decline participation in the daycare or boarding program. Referrals to other facilities that accommodate human-reactive dogs are available upon request.

### What should I bring on my dog's first day?
Please pack your dog's food, treats, any medication, and a blanket or bed from home. Familiar items help dogs feel comfortable and settled throughout the day.

### Can I watch my dog during the day via webcam?
Unfortunately, we do not have webcams available at this time. However, we do have on-site cameras for our staff to monitor activity.
