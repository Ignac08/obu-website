Obedience Unleashed is proud to offer doggy daycare service at our 5 locations. We specialize in personalized care for your dogs, including frequent, positive photo updates and immediate communication if we notice any concerns with your furry family member. Dog daycare consists of socialization in play groups, walks, one-on-one time with staff, play with toys and periods of rest built in throughout the day.

Please note: If your dog is acting aggressive/reactive toward our staff we reserve the right to turn your dog away and not accept into our daycare/boarding program due to liability to our staff. We can give you the names of other boarding facilities that are able to accommodate human reactive dogs.

Payment for daycare is due on the first day of your booking. For example: if you book 6 dates in one booking on Dec. 10, 14, 23, 28, 29, 30 – payment is due for the 6 dates on Dec. 10.  We look forward to welcoming your pup!
