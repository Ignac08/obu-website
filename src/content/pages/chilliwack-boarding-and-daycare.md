## Boarding Dog in Chilliwack

Are you going away for a few days? Would you like to have peace of mind knowing that your dog is cared for and having fun?

We offer a dog boarding program that is tailored to your needs on 5 acres located just off Hwy 1 on the Chilliwack/Abbotsford border. Our spacious 6 ft x 8 ft overnight suites are comfortable and well designed.

The dogs receive many outings throughout the day, walks, supervised social time with other dogs, one-on-one time with our staff every day for training maintenance and bathroom breaks. Their day begins at 6:30am and ends with the last bathroom break/outdoor time at 7pm. Dogs sleep indoors at night. Our facility has a ventilation system to keep it cool in the summer and warm in the winter. We have a nice/spacious play yard with fragrant birch chips where they use the bathroom and have off leash playtime, it is cleaned after every dog uses the bathroom. We also have a 1500 sq ft indoor facility for playtime if it’s raining.
