## A foundation class for puppies and their people

This is our Foundation Puppy Obedience class, designed to help puppies and their owners get off to the right start in training. You get comprehensive class notes after each class, plus a detailed class video if you need to miss one.

Is your puppy jumping on guests, nipping at ankles, chewing household items, digging or running away from you? Over 5 one-hour classes you'll learn to communicate effectively with your puppy and build the life skills that make a healthy, long-lasting relationship.

Classes run in Maple Ridge / Mission (at Silverdale), Chilliwack, Kamloops and Cochrane, AB. Pick your location for dates and local details:
