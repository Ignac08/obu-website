## The next level of obedience

Build on the skills learned in Obedience Bootcamp Level 1. In this class you learn to introduce toys as a reward and a motivator, expanding your training toolbox. It's a proofing class: your dog practises working with distractions through different real-life scenarios.

**Prerequisite:** successful completion of Obedience Unleashed Obedience Bootcamp Level 1 or Puppy Class.

Level 2 runs in [Mission / Maple Ridge](/obedience-unleashed-bootcamp-level-2-mission/), [Chilliwack](/obedience-bootcamp-level-2-chilliwack/) and Cochrane, AB.
