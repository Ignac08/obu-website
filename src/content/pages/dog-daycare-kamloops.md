## Doggie daycare kennel located in Kamloops for your Pets

Would you like to have peace of mind knowing your pup is being cared for in a calm family setting?

We are passionate about doggy daycare and are proud to own a boarding kennel that dogs love.

### Fun daycare kennel services for your pets

We finished building our new boarding facility in the Fall of 2018. It is located on the owners 6 acre property in Barnhartvale, surrounded by peaceful fields, about 100 feet from our house. It is a 80 foot x 25 foot building built to house code, fully insulated, heated, with water, electricity, plumbing, FRP wall covering for hygiene and cleaning, plus a full ventilation system, air conditioning and heat. We have the entire boarding facility professionally cleaned once a day while your furry friend is on vacation. We also accept dogs from the same family.

### Your dogs are our priority

The dogs receive many outings per day during doggie daycare at our kennel. We offer add-ons like walks to explore, supervised social play time with other dogs in play and visit, one-on-one time with our staff every day for training maintenance and bathroom breaks every few hours.  We send updates at least once a week while your dog lives at our kennel. They sleep indoors at night in their own 4 ft x 4 ft suites. We have a fun/spacious yard with fragrant birch chips where your furry friends use the bathroom and have off leash play time or training, it is cleaned after dogs use the bathroom.
