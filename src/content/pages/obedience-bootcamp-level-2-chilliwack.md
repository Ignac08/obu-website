## Obedience Bootcamp Level 2 in Chilliwack

Build on the skills learned in Obedience Bootcamp Level 1 to achieve the next level of obedience. You learn to introduce toys as a reward and a motivator, expanding your training toolbox, and proof your dog's skills around real-life distractions. Classes are taught by our head trainer Deb at 6933 Chadsey Rd.

**Prerequisite:** successful completion of Obedience Unleashed Obedience Bootcamp Level 1 or Puppy Class.
