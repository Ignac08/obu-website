## Puppy training in Kamloops

You will learn to communicate effectively with your puppy to ensure a healthy and long-lasting relationship. Join us for 5 one-hour classes that cover everything you need to know to help your puppy develop important life skills.

Classes are held at our 6-acre property in Barnhartvale, east Kamloops, and are taught by our trainers Heidi and Daymon. Puppies must have their 2nd vaccinations to attend. This is a very popular class that fills up quickly, so book online to reserve your spot.

Obedience Unleashed Dog Training proudly serves Kamloops and surrounding areas.
