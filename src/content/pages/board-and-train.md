## Daycare & Train

Is your dog driving you nuts? Would you like to go away on vacation and come home to an excellently trained dog?

Our board-and-train program specializes in rehabilitation, and successfully deals with canine behaviour problems. By focusing primarily on problem behaviour and difficult dogs, we’ve been able to develop a unique approach that not only transforms dogs, but also transfers seamlessly to the owners.

We pride ourselves on our personalized approach with one-on-one attention paid to each individual dog and situation.

In the board and train, we address all forms of unwanted behavior, including, but not limited to:

- Dog-to-Dog Aggression
- Human Aggression
- Food and/or Resource Guarding
- Separation Anxiety
- Severe Fear Issues
- Reactivity On-Leash

This program also covers all of the basic commands – Sit/Stay, Down/Stay, Place, Heel, Come/Recall – as well as Crate Training, Potty training, Waiting for Food, and Waiting at Thresholds.

Duration of the program: from 3-4 weeks (Please note: shorter programs are available on a case by case basis for dogs that have  completed a training program with our company)

\*If your dog has major behavioural issues, the board and train program will be a minimum of 4 weeks

At Obedience Unleashed Dog Training Inc., each day your dog receives:

- two walks/hikes,
- four personalized training sessions,
- supervised social time with other dogs,

**Our Board and Train requires an Initial consultation prior to starting the program**. After your dog completes the board and train program you receive the following handler classes:

-3 week program: 8 hours of follow up handler training consisting of 1 hour private go-home lesson, 3 hours of group Off Leash Freedom class and 4 Obedience & Socialization group walks.

-4 week program: 10 hours of follow-up handler training consisting of 1 hour private go-home lesson upon pick up, 3 hours of group Off Leash Freedom class and 6 hours of Obedience & Socialization group walks held out and about in town.

 During these sessions we will transfer all of the information, techniques, and strategies to you to ensure continued success. We also offer email and phone support during and after the program is completed.

\* Please contact us so we can help you assess your dog’s needs.

The board and train or daycare and train program is offered at Silverdale (Maple Ridge / Mission), Chilliwack/Abbotsford, Kamloops and Cochrane. It is not offered at Stave Falls.
