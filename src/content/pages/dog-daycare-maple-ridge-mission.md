## Comfortable PET Daycare Services

Are you going on a holiday for a few days and require dog boarding overnight or do you need a pet daycare service during the day?

We offer a special Dog Daycare Mission BC program that is tailored to your needs. Our caring staff treats your dog like family and pups are so excited to return!

### OUR DOG DAYCARE FACILITY

Some of the highlights include large outdoor run for pet socialization, along with 10 acres of forest trails and fields for walks. Our pet accommodations are heated and air conditioned and we send frequent photo updates/communication to owners. Please pack treats so we can reward your dog during the day. Our caring staff have over 20 years of dog handling experience, are Dog Handler Academy certified plus have their pet first aid course.  Contact us to check our facility and schedule a tour any day!

Our goal is to ensure the highest standard of client satisfaction and provide your dog with a true home away from home for the day!
