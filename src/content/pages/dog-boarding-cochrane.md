## Dog boarding in downtown Cochrane

Are you going away for a few days or do you need a doggy daycare service?

We offer a special dog boarding program that is tailored to your needs. Our facility was completed in May 2019 and features a large indoor play area, comfortable suites for the dogs for times of rest and an outdoor area for potty breaks. We are conveniently located in the centre of Cochrane so dogs can enjoy walks to the river and outings around town. Our building has a professional HVAC system and is cleaned daily.

The dogs receive many outings per day, walks, supervised social time with other dogs in play, one-on-one time with our staff and bathroom breaks. A private 30-minute walk can be added to your package for $15. We ask that you provide your dog's food and treats.

Our goal is to ensure the highest standard of client satisfaction and provide your dog with a true home away from home! Contact us to make a reservation today.
