Our dog boarding in Kamloops offers a warm and secure place for your dog to stay while you're gone. We provide each dog an individual suite for the night, take them on daily adventures, have supervised group playtime, and give them one-on-one time with our team. Whether you are planning a weekend away or a longer vacation, our goal is to help your dog feel comfortable throughout their stay.

## Dog boarding in Kamloops

Are you going away for a few days? Would you like to have peace of mind knowing your dog is being cared for in a family setting?

Choosing the best boarding experience for your dog is very important, as each dog has its own unique personality and temperament. We are passionate and committed to giving individual attention and putting your dog's comfort and safety as a priority.

We offer a special dog boarding program that is tailored to your needs. We finished building our new boarding facility in the Fall of 2018. It is located on our 6-acre property in Barnhartvale (just 20 minutes from Kamloops), with plenty of space for your dog to enjoy, surrounded by peaceful, picturesque fields, about 100 feet from our house. It is an 80-foot x 25-foot building built to code, fully insulated and heated, with water, electricity, and plumbing, FRP wall covering for hygiene and cleaning, plus a full ventilation system, air conditioning, and heat. The facility was designed to give dogs a comfortable, secure indoor environment in every season, and it's professionally cleaned once a day.

The dogs receive many outings per day, walks, exercise, supervised group play and social time with other dogs, one-on-one time sessions with our staff every day for training maintenance, and bathroom breaks every few hours. This gives our guests lots of opportunity for activity, enrichment, and individual attention throughout the day.

They sleep indoors at night in their own 4 ft x 4 ft suites. Our facility is climate-controlled with AC, as summer in Kamloops can be intense, and heating as well. We have a nice, spacious yard with fragrant birch chips where they use the bathroom and have fun through off-leash playtime. The area is cleaned after every dog uses the bathroom.

Our team is Dog Handler Academy and Pet First Aid certified for emergencies. Our facility has the necessary license as per Kamloops bylaw, and we require up-to-date immunizations. We regularly communicate with you with photo updates so you can follow along on what your pets are doing.

If you are unsure whether our boarding environment is the right fit for your dog's temperament, try a trial stay. Please reach out prior to booking so we can discuss your pet's requirements, such as personalized feeding schedules or special care. An additional private 15-minute walk can be added to your package for $15.

We ask that you provide your dog's food, treats and favourite toys. Familiar food, treats, and toys can help create a more comfortable routine while your dog is away from home.

For families who need care during the day rather than overnight boarding, full-day [dog daycare](/dog-daycare-kamloops/) is also available at our Kamloops location.
