## Dog Daycare at Silverdale Doggy Vacation Lodge

Are you first time pet parents leaving on vacation out of town or do you need doggy day care services?

We offer a welcoming doggy daycare space at Silverdale Doggy Vacation Lodge for your pet to explore and play. Our team treats your dog with lots of kindness during their stay. Your pet will be sad to leave and so excited to return! Our happy clients recommend our great staff for their outstanding services. The staff will watch for anxiety in your pups and address their behaviour to make sure your pup is happy and safe during play. We truly love and care lots for your dogs like our own and watch them closely in Silverdale. We recommend packing food, lots of treats/cookies and a blanket from home to help your pup settle in to their new place of care. We review your dog’s behaviour change each day and send a regular pup date on social media about your dog’s long, varied and rewarding day.

### Our fun doggy day care services show we care!

Some of the highlights include 3 acres of amazing outdoor yards in Silverdale for your dog to run in, along with a safe 4000 sq ft covered arena for those rainy days, a water park to drop into for sunny days, our dog boarding accommodations are heated and air conditioned, over 20 years of dog handling staff experience, Dog Handler certification, Pet First Aid and much more. Contact us to schedule a tour and see how great our boarding services are for your pup!
