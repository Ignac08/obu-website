## Cochrane puppy class

Is your puppy jumping on guests, nipping at ankles, chewing household items, digging or running away from you? Our Cochrane puppy class gives you the tools to fix it, with trainer Kim.

Classes are held at the Obedience Unleashed Dog Training Centre in Cochrane, Unit 101, 12 Griffin Industrial Point. Puppies must have their 2nd vaccinations to attend. Classes in Cochrane are kept small, with a limit of 5 dogs, so they fill up quickly. Please book online to reserve a spot.

Obedience Unleashed Dog Training proudly serves Cochrane, Calgary and the surrounding area.
