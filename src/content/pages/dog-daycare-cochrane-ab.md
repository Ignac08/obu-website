## Dog daycare and boarding Services in Cochrane

We are proud to offer a special doggy daycare kennel for your fur babies in Cochrane and Calgary areas. Our experienced team is Dog Handler Academy certified and has completed Dog First Aid courses. We will treat your dog like family at our boarding kennel.

### Our dog daycare and boarding facility in Cochrane

Our new dog facility was completed in May 2019 and features a fun indoor play area, comfortable kennels for the dogs for times of rest and an outdoor area for dog potty breaks and fresh air. We are conveniently located in the centre of Cochrane so pets can enjoy walks to the river and outings around town with their friends.
